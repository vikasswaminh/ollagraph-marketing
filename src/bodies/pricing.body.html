FAQ

Pricing, plainly.

What is a credit?

+
One credit = one API call. Most endpoints are 1 credit. A handful cost 3 or 5 (see the table above). You see the exact cost in the response of every call.

What happens if a call fails?

+
You don't pay. Failed calls (status: error) are refunded automatically — the credit goes straight back to your balance, and we log the refund event so you can audit.

Do unused credits roll over?

+
The Free tier's 1,000 credits are a one-time signup grant — they don't refresh. Paid plans auto-topup monthly to your plan's allotment (we set balance to MAX(current_balance, plan_allotment) — you don't lose what you didn't use).

Can I cap spend?

+
Yes. Set hard caps in the dashboard per workspace or per agent. Once you hit the cap, calls return 402 instead of charging. No surprise invoices.

What's the request-per-minute limit for?

+
Plan-based rate limiting — a token bucket that refills continuously. Free is 10 req/min, Starter 30, Pro 60, Business 120, Enterprise 300. Independent from credits, applies even within your monthly allotment.

Why are Bright Data endpoints more expensive?

+
Sites like Amazon, Yelp, and Glassdoor are hard to scrape reliably. We route those through Bright Data's residential network — it costs us money, so we pass it through at 5 credits per call. Everything we self-host stays at 1 credit.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
Credit card on all plans via Stripe. Wire transfer, ACH, and POs on Enterprise. Annual commitments get a discount — talk to us.

Can I self-host?

+
Yes, on Enterprise. We ship Helm charts and Terraform. Customer data and scrape traffic never leave your VPC. Read more →
