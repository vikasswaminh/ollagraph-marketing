HOW CREDITS WORK

FAQ

Pricing, plainly.

What is a credit?

+
One credit = one API call. Most endpoints are 1 credit. The two audit orchestrators (page-audit, competitor-diff) are 3 credits because they fan out into 8–9 probes internally. Add +3 if you opt into the residential proxy surcharge. You see the exact cost in the response of every call.

What happens if a call fails?

+
You don’t pay. Failed calls (status: error) are refunded automatically — the credit goes straight back to your balance, and we log the refund event so you can audit.

Can I just buy credits one-time?

+
Yes. Every pack is sold as both a monthly subscription and a one-time pay-as-you-go purchase, at the same price. PAYG minimum is $5 (one Tiny pack = 5,000 credits). Buy once, use them, walk away — no recurring charge.

Subscription vs one-time — what’s different?

+
Price is identical. Subscription = your pack refills automatically each month. One-time = you buy a pack, the credits sit on your balance, and nothing recurs. Switch between modes anytime in the dashboard.

Do unused credits roll over?

+
The free signup grant (1,000 credits) is one-time and doesn’t refresh. On a subscription we refill to your pack’s allotment each month using MAX(current_balance, pack_allotment) — you don’t lose what you didn’t use. One-time PAYG credits stay on your balance as long as the account is active.

Can I cap spend?

+
Yes. Set hard caps in the dashboard per workspace or per agent. Once you hit the cap, calls return 402 instead of charging. No surprise invoices.

What’s the request-per-minute limit for?

+
Plan-based rate limiting — a token bucket that refills continuously. Tiny and Small are 30 req/min, Medium 60, Large 120. Independent from credits: it caps burst rate, not monthly volume.

Why does the residential proxy cost extra?

+
Residential proxy is opt-in (use_residential_proxy=true) and adds +3 credits to the base cost, because residential bandwidth costs us money. Everything we route through our own fetch path stays at 1 credit.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
Credit card via Dodo Payments — works for both subscriptions and one-time pack purchases. Cancel a subscription anytime from the dashboard; no phone call, no email tag.

What if I need more than 100k credits?

+
Stack Large packs. 5× Large = 500,000 credits for $295. 10× Large = 1,000,000 credits for $590. For teams, the Team and Business plans are cheaper per credit at those volumes and add seats, pooled credits, and governance.

Is the free plan really enough to evaluate?

+
Yes — that’s the point. The free 1,000 credits unlock every endpoint with no card, so a testing team can run a real proof-of-concept across scraping, extraction, intelligence, and the actors before spending anything. It’s a glimpse of the full platform, not a crippled demo.

What do the Team and Business plans add?

+
The same flat credits, plus the org layer: multiple seats on one pooled credit balance, per-key daily caps and budget alerts, an admin audit log, and higher rate limits — with SSO/SCIM, an uptime SLA, and dedicated support on Business. Per credit they cost less than the self-serve packs. Self-serve checkout for these tiers is rolling out; start free and talk to us to switch one on today.
