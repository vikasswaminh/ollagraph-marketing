FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

What is a credit?

+
One credit = one page fetched with the static renderer. Chromium-stealth renders are 4 credits. Each structured extraction is 1 extra credit per page graded.

Do unused credits roll over?

+
Free credits reset monthly. Paid credits roll over for up to 3 months. Enterprise contracts roll over the full term.

Can I cap spend?

+
Yes. Set hard caps per workspace or per agent. We block before charge — never a surprise invoice.

Is observability really free with Developer?

+
Yes — agent traces, MCP visibility, AEO audits, DNS & SSL intel are all included. 14-day retention on Developer, 30-day on Enterprise.

How does the Firecrawl comparison hold up?

+
We benchmark blended per-page price for a typical workload (30% stealth, 60% extract). On low-render workloads we're closer to 35% cheaper; on heavy stealth workloads up to 58%. Receipts in our pricing whitepaper.

What payment methods do you accept?
