We keep this short because there is not much to say: Ollagraph uses only what is strictly necessary to sign you in. We set no advertising, tracking, or cross-site cookies — so there is nothing to opt into and no consent banner to click through.

The one cookie we set

ollagraph_session — set on the app dashboard (app.ollagraph.com) after you sign in. It is a strictly-necessary flag that tells the dashboard you are logged in so it does not flash the sign-in screen on load. It contains no personal data and is not used for tracking. It is removed when you log out.

Local storage (not a cookie)

The dashboard stores your API key in your browser’s localStorage so you stay signed in between visits. It lives only in your browser and is sent only to the Ollagraph API to authenticate your requests. Logging out clears it.

Analytics

Our marketing site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first and cookieless. It sets no cookies, builds no advertising profile, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across other sites.

What we do NOT use

No advertising cookies. No third-party tracking or social pixels. No cross-site profiling. Because we set nothing beyond the strictly-necessary session cookie above, there is no consent or opt-out required under GDPR/ePrivacy — there is simply nothing non-essential to refuse.

Clearing it

Log out of the dashboard to clear the session cookie and stored key, or clear site data for ollagraph.com / app.ollagraph.com in your browser settings at any time.
